# 📦 Frost SMP

Welcome to **Frost SMP**, a custom Minecraft survival experience designed to enhance vanilla gameplay without changing what makes Minecraft fun. Our goal is to create meaningful progression through unique weapons, custom items, and exciting adventure.

# 💎 Custom Items

### 💎 Frost Shard
A rare material obtained from exploration and combat.

### ✨ Frost Token
Crafted from Frost Shards and used for upgrades.

### 📜 Smithing Template
Required to apply upgrades to custom weapons.

### ❄️ Frozen Essence
A rare crafting ingredient for future content.

# 📈 Progression

```text
Explore
   │
   ▼
Collect Frost Shards
   │
   ▼
Craft Frost Token
   │
   ▼
Find Smithing Template
   │
   ▼
Upgrade Weapon
   │
   ▼
Unlock New Ability
   │
   ▼
Master Your Weapon
```

### Upgrade Steps

1. Explore the world.
2. Obtain Frost Shards.
3. Craft a Frost Token.
4. Find a Smithing Template.
5. Upgrade your weapon.
6. Unlock stronger abilities.
7. Continue upgrading until fully mastered.
